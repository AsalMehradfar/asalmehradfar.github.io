Asal Mehradfar (she/her)

Ph.D. Candidate, University of Southern California

I develop machine learning methods that accelerate scientific discovery by combining modern AI with domain expertise. My research focuses on developing AI for engineering and scientific discovery, with applications in analog and RF circuit design, lipid nanoparticle engineering, and reliable generative AI.

Advised by Prof. Salman Avestimehr.

Research Areas

Developing machine learning methods for engineering, biomedical, and scientific discovery.

AI-Driven Analog and RF Circuit Design

Machine learning for circuit benchmarking, topology-aware modeling, inverse design, electromagnetic modeling, physical synthesis, and automated layout generation.

AI-Driven Lipid Nanoparticle Design

Predictive, interpretable, and generative machine learning for lipid nanoparticle (LNP) transfection, biodistribution, and targeted therapeutic delivery.
